# ADR#002

## Topic

Choose naming options for break from work for any reason

## Problem

There are currently two naming options for break actions:

- `step-in` and `step-out` : The term step-out may be confused with check-out, as both can imply that the user is leaving work. This could lead to user mistakes when performing attendance actions.
- `break-start` and `break-end` : The term break implies that the user is taking a rest. However, leaving the workplace does not necessarily mean taking a break, as the user may leave for work-related or personal activities.
- `away` and `return` : These terms are more neutral and clearly describe the actions of leaving and returning to the workplace, regardless of the reason.

![alt text](https://absensi-docs.r2.lab.biz.id/attendances/attendance-workflow.webp){.landscape-img}

## Decision

Use `away` and `return` as the standard names for break actions.

## Reasoning

Leaves activities are distinct from checking in and checking out. Using away and return makes the purpose of each action clearer and reduces the risk of confusion with other attendance actions.
